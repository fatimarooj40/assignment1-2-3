About the summit

The summit brings university students, working developers and local startup teams together for one day in Islamabad. There are 12 talks on four topics, and every attendee can join workshops in the afternoon. We have 400 seats, so register early.

Please bring your own laptop with a code editor installed. If you are new to HTML and CSS, the free MDN guides are a good place to start before the workshop.

How the day runs

  1. Registration and tea, 8:30 AM
  2. Opening keynote, 9:30 AM
  3. Talks and workshops, 11:00 AM to 3:30 PM
  4. Lunch and project demos, 1:00 PM
  5. Closing panel and prize draw, 4:00 PM

Topics covered

  • Web development basics
  • Cybersecurity at home and at work
  • Cloud and deployment
  • Career advice and CV reviews
